# Harvest

GUIDE.md step 9. An MV is a copy of the template, so what it taught stays in the copy unless someone brings it back.

When the MV is done, list what could come back. Start from the Log's Harvest candidates (`mv/LOG.md`), then compare the copy with the template (`diff -r` over `engine/`, `styles/`, `tools/` and `docs/`; what the Chapters and their briefs had to work around; what the person changed their mind about after seeing it): engine and Style Profile changes, rules learned the hard way, tools made for this MV.

Put each item to the person as its own question (bring it back as it is, generalised, or not), then make the chosen ones on a branch of the template and show them before anything is merged. Note what they chose, and what they turned down, in the MV's Log.
