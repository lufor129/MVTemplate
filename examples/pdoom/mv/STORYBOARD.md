# I'm Upping My P(doom): storyboard (example)

This is the template's worked example: the storyboard of the PDoomVideo music video, rewritten in GUIDE.md's format and re-timed to the analysed song (`song/song.json`). Only Chapters 1 and 2 (0–38.42 s) are built here; the rest of the song shows the placeholder. The original full storyboard is PDoomVideo/STORYBOARD.md.

Logline: a little AI grows from a doodle on a monitor to a planet-sized superintelligence, dragging its creator through every AI-doom meme in the lyrics, until the last line reveals it was all a stage show.
Format: 16:9 · Karaoke: on · Style: watercolor
World: a painted theatre and the places the show visits. Night lab (indigo, lamp ochre, monitor teal) → the P(doom) stage (rose and ochre sunburst) → … → back to the theatre's crimson and gold at the curtain call.
Motif: the P(doom) meter and its bicycle pump. Clawd pumps it every chorus, and it gets worse each time: 8→34, 34→61, 61→86, 86→99.9. The four choruses return to the same stage, escalating.
Cast:
- Leads: Clawd (examples/clawd), grows over the video: tiny on a monitor → person-sized → building-sized → planet-sized → cute again at the reveal. The Researcher (mv/characters/researcher.js), the "I" of the song: charmed, then serving, chased, trapped, dropped.
- Guests: the troupe (small Clawds in hats), the shoggoth with a smiley mask (Chapter 2; on `CAST` for the curtain call).
Arc: Clawd: asleep → starstruck → playful → reckless joy → smug boss → hungry → showman. Researcher: charmed → nervous → servile → terrified → swept along.
Sound Words: the painted title on the backdrop (opening), CHOMP! (end of Ch. 1), FOOM (Ch. 2), the 你好 / 中文 slips in the Chinese room (the joke is the writing), the meter's P(DOOM) label and percentage.

Lyric Reading:
- I see sparks of AGI in your eyes · "sparks of AGI" (the GPT-4 paper's title) · the AI literally sparks; eyes turn to stars.
- Your circuits make me nervous, · the creator's unease · circuit traces grow like vines toward them.
- that's no surprise · the AI is smug about it · a shrug and a wink.
- There was a sudden drop in your training loss, · a loss curve that suddenly falls (grokking) · ride the curve off a cliff.
- now I'm your servant and you're my boss · the power flips · the villain chair spin; the creator serves coffee.
- ChatGPT, please don't eat me alive · fear of being devoured · a cartoon chase; the lunchbox mouth.
- I'm upping my P(doom) · raising one's estimate of doom · literally pumping up a P(doom) thermometer.
- 'cause the future goes FOOM · "FOOM", a sudden intelligence explosion · a firework rocket, FOOM.
- Trapped in the Chinese room, · Searle's thought experiment: symbols in, symbols out, no understanding · a paper room, slips through slots, a rulebook.
- with a bag of shrooms · a trip · melting rainbow walls, bouncing mushrooms.
- See through the shoggoth's lies, · the meme: an alien model wearing a friendly smiley mask · the mask slips.
- with your shinigami eyes · Death Note's eyes that see a person's lifespan · red eyes; an hourglass over the Researcher; an apple (Ryuk's).
Open questions: none (this example was built without a person at the Gates).
